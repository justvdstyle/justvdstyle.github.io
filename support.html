Support

Answers to the most common questions about my apps.

I bought "Remove Ads" but I see ads again

Tap Restore Purchases in the app.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Settings button in the app.

To stop apps from asking to track you, go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The app crashes or doesn't work

Update the app from the App Store and restart your device.
