Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy covers all iPhone and iPad apps published on the App Store by Viktor Dobrev ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in Bulgaria. Contact: viktordbv@gmail.com.

In short
  • I don't run servers, and I don't collect, sell or see your personal data.
  • Anything you create in an app (lists, scores, settings) stays on your device.
  • Free apps show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you choose whether ads are personalized. On iOS, the app asks your permission before using your device's advertising identifier.
  • Purchases go through Apple. I never receive your payment details.

1. Data that stays on your device

The apps save your content and settings (for example, your own lists, answers, high scores and whether you bought "Remove Ads") only on your device, using standard iOS storage. This data is not sent to me. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free apps show banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking; otherwise an identifier for the app vendor (IDFV)Ads, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itShowing relevant ads, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (views, taps) and app usage related to adsMeasurement and reporting

Google acts under its own privacy policy. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept or refuse personalized ads, and change your choice later in the app's menu under Privacy Settings. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • Tracking (all countries): iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: where offered, a one-time purchase turns off ads in that app.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases and paid apps are processed by Apple. I receive only anonymous sales reports from Apple, never your name, Apple Account or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you.

5. Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

Ads data is processed by Google based on your consent where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), and otherwise based on legitimate interest in funding free apps through advertising. Because I don't hold your personal data, requests to access or delete ad data should go to Google. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg).
